Pino's Jams

patches from the rack beside my piano, written as patterns. press play to hear one the way my rack plays it, or open it in strudel to hear it properly.

what is on the rack
sequencer: steps through the notes in time; the notes and rests themselves
oscillator: the raw tone, a wave shape: sawtooth, square, triangle or sine
filter: takes the top off the tone; a cutoff in hertz, low is dark, high is bright
envelope: how each note starts and how it lets go, in seconds
clock: the pace: slow the whole pattern down or run it faster
amplifier: how loud it comes out, nought to one
